# Commit graph and commit menu

Read this when touching `gittan.commitgraph`, `gittan.graph`, `gittan.pickaxe`, `umain.commitactions.inc`. The Files page Browse Files... opens is in files-page.md.

- The commit graph (View > Commit Graph, Cmd+Option+3, Ctrl+Shift+3 elsewhere, or Commit Graph in the list a click on the status bar's "↑2 ↓5" drops up) is `TGittanCommitGraph` in `gittan.commitgraph.pp`, a panel in the middle of the form (`FGraph`, a child of the form like the popup lists, closed by Escape, its close button, a click outside it, except on its `Keeper` (the sidebar's rows, so that the Graph row can toggle it), or the application going to the background through the same user input hook, every close reported by `OnClose`; `Recenter` from `EditorPanelResize` keeps it in the middle). `ShowCommitGraph` in `umain.actions.inc` reads the newest `GraphLimit` (500) commits with `TGittanRepo.GraphCommits` (`git log -z --topo-order --decorate=full` over `--branches --remotes --tags HEAD`, the decorations named with `--decorate-refs` since git leaves `refs/bisect` out by default, with the parents, `%D` and the body last, since it may hold line breaks, marked `Unpushed` and `Unpulled` from `rev-list @{upstream}..HEAD` and `HEAD..@{upstream}`; tested) and pops it up; a second click closes it. Each row has the commit's dot in its lane (a ring around it on the commit HEAD is on), its half of the lines through the row, the refs as chips (the current branch filled in the accent, other branches outlined in it, remote branches dimmed, tags in amber, a bisect's marks as bad in red, good in green and skip dimmed (`grBisect`); `ParseGraphRefs` reads `%D` and drops a remote's HEAD and the stash), the subject, the author and age, an arrow (↑ not pushed in the accent, ↓ not pulled) and the short id; only the rows in view are painted. Up, Down, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move, Enter or a click picks, and `GraphPick` shows the commit against its parent through `ShowBlameCommit` and goes to the summary (`ShowSummary`), from the Repository page too, where its card names it. The pick keeps the rows the filter showed, newest first (`ShownHashes`, in `FGraphTrail`), and what the search field held (`FilterText`, `FGraphTrailFilter`): Go > Older Commit (F9) and Newer Commit (Shift+F9), and Older › and ‹ Newer at the right of the card's author line (`TGittanSummary.SetCommitSteps`, `OnCommitStep`), step to the next row of them (`CommitStepHash`, `GoToCommitStep`), so a search's commits are read one after another without the graph; off them (a commit from the blame band, or the last row) Older goes to the first parent and Newer nowhere, and an item or link that leads nowhere is off (`UpdateCommitSteps`, from `ApplySnapshot`). The graph opened again while one of them is shown comes back with that search and on that commit (`Popup`'s `AKeep`), not on HEAD. The lanes come from `LayoutGraph` in `gittan.graph.pp` (no LCL, tested in `tests/testgraph.pp`): every lane waits for the commit it leads to next; a commit takes the leftmost lane waiting for it, or the lowest free one at a tip, and the other lanes waiting for it end in it; its first parent goes straight down unless a lane left of it waits for that parent, which it then joins, and each further parent goes to the lane waiting for it or a new one. A row is two halves of straight lines, `TopEdges` and `BottomEdges`, lane to lane; a lane keeps its color, taken in turn from the history view's six swatch hues (`SetLaneColors` from `ApplyTheme`), and lanes past `MaxLanes` (12) draw at the last. The header holds a search field (a native `TEdit`, focused when the graph opens; Up, Down, Page Up, Page Down and Enter in it go to the rows, Home and End while it is empty, and Escape empties it before it closes the panel): what is typed is read by `ParseGraphFilter` in `gittan.graph.pp` (tested in `tests/testgraph.pp`) as words every commit must have in its subject, body, author or id, and `author:`, `since:` (or `after:`), `until:` (or `before:`), `path:` and `change:` parts, a quoted phrase kept together, a date as 2024, 2024-06 or 2024-06-15 (until includes its day, month or year; a date not readable yet while it is typed is ignored, as is a part with nothing after the colon). The matching is the graph's own (`ApplyFilter`, `FilterDelay` (200 ms) after the last keystroke, `MatchesGraphFilter` over texts and words folded with `UTF8LowerCase`, the authors composed as the history view composes them), so typing filters at once; only the commits touching a `path:` come from git, through `OnPathCommits` (`GraphPathCommits`, `TGittanRepo.PathCommits`: `git --literal-pathspecs log --format=%H` over the same refs, with `--follow` for a file, so it follows renames, not for a folder; tested), asked again only when the paths change and kept in a sorted list (`FPathHashes`). A `change:` part keeps the commits whose diff adds or removes a text: `PickaxeOption` in `gittan.pickaxe.pp` (no LCL, tested in `tests/testpickaxe.pp`) makes it `git log -S<text>` (the commits that change how many times the text occurs: the one that added a line and the one that took it away, not one that only moved it) or, for a value between slashes (`change:/Cmd\+P/`), `git log -G<regex>` (every commit with an added or removed line matching it, moves too; as with plain `git log`, a merge is not searched, so a change made only in a merge's conflict resolution is not found, since `-m` would find every merge that brings a change in), with `--regexp-ignore-case` unless the text has a capital letter (a letter after a backslash in a regular expression does not count); a slash on one side only is text. `ChangeCommits` runs one log per `change:` part over the same refs, at most `GraphSearchLimit`, and keeps the commits every part found. Since git reads every diff for it, which takes seconds on a large repository, it runs in a `TGittanChangeJob`: the graph raises `OnChangeCommits` when the changes asked for differ from those found (`FChangeKey`, `FChangeDone`), and `GraphChangeCommits` gives up the job still running (`AbandonChangeJob`: `Stop` ends its git through the job's `Cancelled`, and the job goes to `FBlameZombies`) and starts one; with none to search for (the filter cleared, or back to the changes found) it is raised with none, which only gives the job up. `change://` (an empty pattern on the way to a regex) is left out like an empty `change:`; `ChangeJobDone` answers with `SetChangeCommits`, which drops an answer for changes no longer asked for. Until the answer comes no commit is shown and the list says "Searching the history for the change..."; a search git refused (a regular expression it cannot read) says "git could not search:" with git's reason. The job is also given up when the graph closes (`GraphClosed`) and when the frame goes (`StopBlame`), and `Popup` forgets what was found, since the repository may have changed. A filter shows the matching commits as a flat list (`FShown` maps the rows to `FCommits`; a dot in one lane and no lines, since the lanes of a part of the history mean nothing; the rows control stays at `MaxRowsHeight` (32000 px, since the LCL sends a control's size in 16 bits) and `RowShift` maps the scroll position onto the rows when there are more, the box subclassed as `TGittanGraphBox` so a scroll repaints them), the highlighted commit kept when it is still shown, and a last row says "n of m commits match". Since a filter looks through the whole history, a graph that was cut asks the form for all of it once (`OnMore`; `GraphMore` runs `GraphCommits` with `GraphSearchLimit` (50000) and answers with `Replace`, which keeps the filter and the highlight), as a click on the last row ("Showing the newest 500 commits · Load all", in the accent while it can be clicked) does; `ShowCommitGraph(AFilter)` with a filter loads that many at once. Show History in the file menu (`FFileHistoryItem`: a file of the tree, the summary or the header, or a folder of the tree through `FContextFolder`) and Go > File History (the shown file) open the graph with `path:<path>` (`PathFilter`, quoted when the path has a space): the log of a file or folder. A commit can be marked for a comparison from its menu (see below); the marked one's short id is drawn bold in the accent (`Marked`). The header's title is followed by the current branch and how far it is from its upstream (↑ not pushed, ↓ not pulled, or in sync), which end before the Statistics toggle: a long branch name is cut with an ellipsis (`FitText`), and the upstream part is left out when it leaves the name less than six characters' room. The Statistics toggle left of the search field (`FStatsRect`, `SetStatsOn`, filled in the accent while on, off again at each `Popup`) shows, in the place of the rows, the statistics of the commits the rows would show (the filter applies): `CommitStats` in `gittan.commitstats.pp` (no LCL, tested in `tests/testcommitstats.pp`) counts them per week (Monday to Sunday in local time, the zone's offset passed in, `WeekStart`) from the oldest commit's week to the newest's, as many weeks as fit at `MinWeekWidth` (3 px) each, the newest kept, and per author (merged by `NameKey` as the repository page does, spelled as their newest commit, the most commits first, ties in the order of their newest commit) with each author's weeks. `PaintStats` draws a line with the counts and the dates (or, for the week under the mouse, its count and the `WeekAuthors` (3) authors with the most commits in it), a bar for every week stacked by author (the authors with the most commits in the lane colors, the others dim), the first and last week's dates, then a row per author with a swatch, the name, the count and their weeks as small bars against the most one author made in a week; only the rows in view are painted. A click on an author's row sets the search to `author:<name>` (the paths and changes of the filter kept) and goes back to the rows; the keys scroll while it is on. Turned on while the log was cut, it asks for the whole log first (`OnMore`), as a filter does.
- The commit menu (`umain.commitactions.inc`): a right click on a row of the commit graph, or the Menu key on the highlighted one, raises `TGittanCommitGraph.OnContext` with the commit and a screen point, and `GraphContext` builds `FCommitPopup` for that commit: Show Commit, Compare with Working Tree (`SetBase`), Mark for Comparison (Unmark on the marked one; `FMarkHash`, `FMarkName`, the name the commit goes by, and `FMarkDate`; the graph stays open and marks the row; the mark lasts until another repository is opened) and, with another commit marked, Compare with Marked <name>, which sets the base to the range from the older of the two to the newer by their times, so the views show what changed between them, labeled by the range itself ("v1..main"), Switch to each local branch on it but the current one (`SwitchBranch`), Create Branch Here... (asks a name, `CreateBranch` with `switch -c`, so the branch is only made when the switch works), Create Tag Here... (a name and an optional message, which makes it annotated), Merge <name> into <branch> (`MergeRef`, `merge --no-edit`), Cherry-pick into <branch>, Revert Commit (`revert --no-edit`), Rebase Interactively from Here... (the interactive rebase, see branches-stash-tags.md), Reset <branch> to Here with Soft, Mixed and Hard, Bisect (see branches-stash-tags.md) (`ResetAsk` asks first, saying what each keeps and which commits leave the branch: those after the commit when it is in HEAD's history, none when HEAD is in its history (forward, as from the reflog, where only Hard notes a loss), else those HEAD has that it lacks; HEAD is named while it is on no branch, `ResetTarget`; a hard reset keeps untracked files, as git does), Copy Commit Hash and, with gh, Open Commit on GitHub. The commit goes by its local branch, else a remote one, else a tag, else its short id. Merge and cherry-pick are off for a commit already in HEAD's history (`IsAncestor`, `merge-base --is-ancestor`), revert for one not in it, cherry-pick and revert for a merge commit (git wants its mainline), and everything that moves HEAD while git is in the middle of something or a commit, fetch, pull or push runs. The graph closes on a pick; after an action that changes the history it opens again with the result. Merge, cherry-pick and revert run with `GIT_EDITOR=:` (`RunCommitting` in `gittan.git.pp`); one that stops with conflicts says so in the status bar (`AConflicts`) and leaves them to the Commit section. The watcher is stopped meanwhile, as for a switch. Names in captions get their ampersands doubled (`Amp`). Tested in `tests/testgit.pp` (`TestCommitActions`, `TestCommitActionConflicts`).
- A commit can be dragged onto the row of the commit HEAD is on to merge it (`TGittanCommitGraph.OnDrop`, `GraphDrop` in `umain.commitactions.inc`): the left button down on a row (`TGittanGraphRows.MouseDown`, `FPressRow`) starts a drag once the mouse is `DragThreshold` (5 px) away (`DragMove`), so a click that shakes a little still picks; meanwhile the pressed row stays highlighted, HEAD's row (`IsHeadRow`, not the pressed one) is outlined in the accent while the mouse is over it within the box (`FDropRow`, `SetDropRow`, `DropRowAt`: none outside the box, where a captured release still comes) with `crDrag` as the cursor, `crNoDrop` elsewhere, the box scrolls half a row every `DragScrollInterval` (40 ms) while the mouse is within a row of its top or bottom, or past them (`FDragTimer`, `DragTimerTick`), and Escape gives the drag up (`GiveUpDrag`, also when the rows are replaced; `EndDrag` on `Dismiss`), and the release that follows picks nothing (`FDragGivenUp`). It works in a filter's flat list too. Let go on HEAD's row, `GraphDrop` shows `FCommitPopup` with the commit menu's own Merge <name> into <branch> (`caMerge`, off while `RepoBusy`), so a drag let go by mistake does nothing, or "<name> is already in <branch>", disabled, for a commit HEAD's history has; the graph stays open under it. The name is `CommitKnownName`, as in the commit menu. Merge, from the drop or the menu, merges that name when it is still the commit (`CommitId`), so git's message says "Merge branch 'feature'", else the id.
- Browse Files... in the commit menu, the tag menu and the reflog's menu (`BrowseCommit`) shows the commit's files on the Files page, see files-page.md; it changes nothing, but the page takes the box, so the commit graph closes.
