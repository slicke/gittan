# Application icon

Read this when touching the artwork, `make icons`, the .ico/.icns/PNGs or how the macOS bundle gets its icon.

- The artwork comes finished, a crossing red and green strand with a ring at each end on a dark rounded tile, drawn as SVG and exported to PNG by hand; nothing in the build draws or sizes it. `icons/gittan-mac.svg` and its export `icons/gittan-mac.png` (1024 pixels) are the macOS icon, the tile already on Apple's 824/1024 grid with its own drop shadow. `icons/gittan-linux.svg` (the larger sizes) and `icons/gittan-linux-small.svg` (a simpler drawing for the small ones) give `icons/png/gittan-<n>.png` at 16, 22, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and 512 for Windows and Linux, the tile filling more of its canvas. `make icons` runs `tools/makeicons.swift` (macOS only: CoreGraphics and `iconutil`), which packs them: `icons/Gittan.icns` from `gittan-mac.png` scaled to each size, `Gittan.ico` from the PNGs of 16 to 256 as they are (it refuses one of the wrong size). The strands start and end under their rings (`V62`/`V194` with a few units more, in every SVG, the wordmark's too), so no gap shows between a ring and its strand. macOS 26 and later draw an .icns that is not their own shape on a grey tile of their own, which showed in Cmd-Tab; for them `icons/Gittan.icon` is an Icon Composer document, the tile's gradient as its fill and the mark as one layer (`Assets/mark.png`, Chrome's export of `icons/gittan-mark.svg`: the mark of `gittan-mac.svg` alone on the 1024 canvas, at the size it has on the tile, without its shadow), which the system shapes, shadows and glazes itself; `make icons` also compiles it with Xcode's `actool` (`ACTOOL`: `/Applications/Xcode.app`'s, else the one `xcrun --find actool` names, as for an Xcode installed elsewhere; it needs an absolute path) into `icons/Assets.car`. A new artwork replaces those files and runs `make icons`. The PNGs are Chrome's exports: headless, a page with the SVG as an `<img>` of the size, `--default-background-color=00000000`, scale factor 1 (it gives the committed files pixel for pixel). Commit the generated files; other platforms only consume them. The Linux release copies `icons/png/gittan-256.png` beside the binary as `gittan.png`.
- `Gittan.ico` beside `Gittan.lpi` is the project icon (`<Icon Value="0"/>` in the .lpi means it is set); Lazarus compiles it into the executable's resources, which the LCL uses for the window and taskbar icon on Windows and Linux.
- Lazarus creates `Gittan.app` without an icon, so the Darwin build targets in the Makefile copy `icons/Gittan.icns` and `icons/Assets.car` into `Contents/Resources` and add `CFBundleIconFile` and `CFBundleIconName` (Gittan, the .car's icon) to `Info.plist` after lazbuild. The `Info.plist` lazbuild writes into a new bundle has the project title as `CFBundleName` with a bare `&`, which PlistBuddy refuses to read (so CI, which always starts without a bundle, shipped no icon); the targets escape it, set `CFBundleName` to Gittan and check the result with `plutil -lint`. A bundle built from the Lazarus IDE lacks the icon until the next `make`. Finder caches icons; `touch Gittan.app` or a relaunch of Finder refreshes it.
- The dialogs' icon is the braid graph alone, without the application icon's tile: `icons/gittan-dialog.svg` (the red and the green strand with their rings, the crossing's gap a mask in the red strand rather than a white stroke, so it sits on a dark dialog as on a light one, and its viewBox cropped to the drawing, 18 18 220 220, so the mark fills its box as a system icon does), rendered with Chrome headless with a transparent background to `icons/dialog/gittan-dialog-48.png`, `-96.png` and `-144.png` (an `<img>` of the SVG at that size, `--default-background-color=00000000 --force-device-scale-factor=1 --window-size=<n>,<n>`). `make dialogicon` turns them into byte arrays in `src/ui/gittan.dialogicon.inc` (kept in git, so a build needs neither the rule nor od and awk), which `gittan.dialogicon.pp` reads with `DialogIcon`, the PNG for the size in device pixels, read once. Gittan's dialogs (`gittan.askdlg`) draw it for a question, a note or an input, and the notice (`gittan.notice`) over its title; About keeps the application icon.
