# Command palette

Read this when touching `umain.palette.inc`, `gittan.paletteside`, Go > Command Palette..., `PaletteRecent` in the settings, or a menu item the palette should leave out.

- Go > Command Palette... (Cmd+P, Ctrl+P elsewhere; `FPaletteItem`) shows `FPalettePopup`, a `TGittanPopupList` shown with `PopupCentered` across the top of the frame, under the title bar's strip (`FTitleBarHeight`), with its filter field focused ("Type an action"). Its rows are the actions the menus offer right now, so a new menu item shows up in it with nothing more to do: `ListPaletteActions` walks `FMainMenu` (and `FAppMenuItem` on macOS, as "Gittan") and takes every item that is visible, enabled, has a caption and an `OnClick`, by its menu path with the hotkey ampersands stripped ("Branch: Switch Branch...", "View: Folding: Fold All"), with its shortcut as the detail (`ShortcutLabel`) and checked as in its menu. Before a submenu is walked its own `OnClick` runs, the handler that brings its items up to date as it opens (`FileMenuClick`, `BranchMenuOpen`, `StashMenuOpen`, `ViewMenuClick`, `ToolsMenuClick`), so a disabled action is left out. View > Syntax (hundreds of lexers) and the palette's own item are left out.
- The actions last run from the palette come first under "Recently used", the rest under "Other actions" in menu order; the list is `TGittanSettings.PaletteRecent` (section `Palette`, at most `MaxPaletteRecent`, newest first, `AddPaletteRecent`; tested in `tests/testsettings.pp`), kept by name, so a name no longer offered is skipped. Typing keeps the rows that have every word typed in their name, in any order ("push force"), the headers dropped.
- `FPaletteNames` holds the name of each row (the row's tag is its index there). A pick lists the actions again and clicks (`TMenuItem.Click`) the item of that name, so a menu rebuilt while the palette was open (Open Recent, Compare With) leaves no dangling item, and an action that became disabled meanwhile does nothing.
- Buttons beside the palette (`ShowPaletteSides`, after `PopupCentered`): while it is open, `FPaletteLeft` holds Undo, Redo and Stash and `FPaletteRight` Pull, Push and New Branch, each a `TGittanPaletteSide` (`gittan.paletteside.pp`): a column of tiles with the status bar's icon (`DrawStatusIcon`; `HintUndo` and `HintRedo` are an arc with an arrowhead at its left or right end) over a short name, in the popup lists' colors with a border, the fill under the mouse on an enabled tile, `Px(10)` from the palette's sides and level with its top. A tile is the menu item itself (`FUndoItem`, `FRedoItem`, `FStashAllItem`, `FPullItem`, `FPushItem`, `FNewBranchItem`): dimmed while it is disabled (the palette has just run the menus' open handlers), its hint the item's whole caption with its shortcut ("Undo Commit "Fix" "), and a click (on release over the tile pressed) closes the palette and clicks the item (`PaletteSideClick`). Both are the palette's keepers (`TGittanPopupList.AddKeeper`, `TGittanPopupHook.AddKeeper` beside its `Keeper`), so a press on them leaves it open until the click; the palette's `OnClose` (Escape, a pick, a click outside) hides them (`PalettePopupClose`). A frame too narrow for a column beside the palette shows neither.
- The palette is the frame's: the forge view has a menu of its own without it.
