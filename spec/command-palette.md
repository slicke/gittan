# Command palette

Read this when touching `umain.palette.inc`, Go > Command Palette..., `PaletteRecent` in the settings, or a menu item the palette should leave out.

- Go > Command Palette... (Cmd+P, Ctrl+P elsewhere; `FPaletteItem`) shows `FPalettePopup`, a `TGittanPopupList` shown with `PopupCentered` across the top of the frame, under the title bar's strip (`FTitleBarHeight`), with its filter field focused ("Type an action"). Its rows are the actions the menus offer right now, so a new menu item shows up in it with nothing more to do: `ListPaletteActions` walks `FMainMenu` (and `FAppMenuItem` on macOS, as "Gittan") and takes every item that is visible, enabled, has a caption and an `OnClick`, by its menu path with the hotkey ampersands stripped ("Branch: Switch Branch...", "View: Folding: Fold All"), with its shortcut as the detail (`ShortcutLabel`) and checked as in its menu. Before a submenu is walked its own `OnClick` runs, the handler that brings its items up to date as it opens (`FileMenuClick`, `BranchMenuOpen`, `StashMenuOpen`, `ViewMenuClick`, `ToolsMenuClick`), so a disabled action is left out. The Syntax menu (hundreds of lexers) and the palette's own item are left out.
- The actions last run from the palette come first under "Recently used", the rest under "Other actions" in menu order; the list is `TGittanSettings.PaletteRecent` (section `Palette`, at most `MaxPaletteRecent`, newest first, `AddPaletteRecent`; tested in `tests/testsettings.pp`), kept by name, so a name no longer offered is skipped. Typing keeps the rows that have every word typed in their name, in any order ("push force"), the headers dropped.
- `FPaletteNames` holds the name of each row (the row's tag is its index there). A pick lists the actions again and clicks (`TMenuItem.Click`) the item of that name, so a menu rebuilt while the palette was open (Open Recent, Compare With) leaves no dangling item, and an action that became disabled meanwhile does nothing.
- The palette is the frame's: the forge view has a menu of its own without it.
